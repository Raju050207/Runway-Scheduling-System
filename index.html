Assigns arriving and departing flights to runways so no two use the same runway within the safety gap, and emergencies go first.

How the scheduler works

  1. Every flight is inserted into a custom max-heap (CustomPriorityQueue::push) ordered by: emergency status, then priority, then earlier requested time, then shorter runway occupation (higherPriority). Insert is O(log n).
  2. The highest-priority flight is popped from the heap (CustomPriorityQueue::pop), also O(log n).
  3. For each open runway, the earliest available slot at or after the requested time that still keeps the separation buffer from every existing operation on that runway is found (findAvailableTime).
  4. The runway producing the smallest delay is chosen (scheduleAircraft) — a greedy choice, not an exhaustive search over all orderings.
  5. After every flight is placed, every runway is re-checked for overlaps or separation violations (auditConflicts).

Overall complexity is O(n log n) for the heap operations plus O(n · r · k) for slot-finding, where n = flights, r = runways, k = operations already on a runway.
